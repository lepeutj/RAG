# Delivery Policy

Standard delivery is available in the United Kingdom, France, Germany, Spain, and Italy. Orders are prepared within one business day and standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days after dispatch.

## Express delivery

Express delivery is available only for orders placed before 14:00 Central European Time on business days. It is not available for personalized products or deliveries to remote areas. Eligible express orders arrive within 1 to 2 business days after dispatch.

## Tracking and delayed orders

Customers receive a tracking link by email after dispatch. If an order has not arrived 7 business days after the estimated delivery date, customer support can open a carrier investigation.
