# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of purchase, provided the item is returned in its original condition with proof of purchase.

## Exceptions

Personalized and perishable products are not eligible for a refund. Original delivery charges are not refunded unless the item is confirmed to be defective.

## Process

1. Contact customer support at support@example.com.
2. Provide the order number and reason for the return.
3. Receive a prepaid return label within 48 hours.
4. Refunds are issued within 5 to 10 business days after the warehouse receives the returned package.
