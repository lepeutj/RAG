# Subscription Policy

Subscriptions renew automatically on the same calendar day each month. The renewal price and billing date are shown in the customer account before the first payment and before each renewal.

## Cancellation

Customers can cancel from **Account > Subscription** at any time. Cancellation stops the next renewal; access remains available until the end of the current billed period. A cancellation request made after a renewal payment does not automatically refund that payment.

## Plan changes

Upgrades take effect immediately and the remaining value of the current plan is credited toward the new plan. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.
